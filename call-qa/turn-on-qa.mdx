---
title: "Turn on QA"
description: "Turn on QA scoring for your Klariqo account yourself: what you need first, what changes, how calls are charged afterwards, and how to turn it off."
---

Every account gets signed records. QA adds a score against your scorecard, and sentiment, to each call. Your account owner or an admin can turn it on.

## What you need first

<Steps>
  <Step title="A balance">
    QA is paid, so your balance must be above $0. See [your balance and top-ups](/billing/balance-and-top-ups).
  </Step>
  <Step title="A scorecard">
    QA scores calls against your rules, so you need an active scorecard. Open **Scorecard** and write your own checks, or start from **Load a starter preset**. Presets are editable suggestions, not legal templates. See [scorecards](/call-qa/scorecards).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn it on

Go to **Billing**, choose **Turn on QA**, read the price, and confirm.

## What changes

- **Calls received after you turn it on are scored.** Earlier calls stay as records and aren't charged as QA.
- **Each scored call is charged at the QA price, with the record included.** Your remaining free records don't cover scored calls.
- **You only pay the QA price for a real score.** A call that couldn't be scored is treated as a record, at the record price or as a free record if you have any left. See [how charging works](/billing/how-charging-works).
- **If your balance runs out,** new calls wait until you add funds. See [your balance and top-ups](/billing/balance-and-top-ups).

## Turn it off

Go to **Billing**, choose **Turn off QA**, and confirm.

- **New calls get a signed record and transcript,** with no score or sentiment. They use any free records you have left, then the record price.
- **A call scored while QA was on is still charged as QA,** even if it's charged after you turn QA off. Past calls keep their scores.
- **Paid calls don't use your free records,** so you keep the free records you had.
- **You can turn QA back on at any time.** You need a balance and a scorecard, as before.

<Warning>
  QA shows how a call matched your own rules. It is not a legal judgment, and a passing score does not make a call lawful.
</Warning>
