---
title: "Get started"
description: "Sign up, connect your VICIdial, and get signed records of your calls with no call with us, then turn on QA scoring when you want it."
---

You can do all of this yourself. You need a VICIdial with call recording on, and an API user for it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.klariqo.com](https://app.klariqo.com). No card is needed. Your first 10,000 recorded-call records are free, once, with no expiry date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your dialer">
    Enter your VICIdial address and API user, then run **Test connection**. See [Connect VICIdial](/dialer-integrations/connect-vicidial). If a step fails, [fix a failed connection test](/dialer-integrations/fix-a-connection-test) explains each result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your first records">
    Klariqo picks up new recordings from your dialer and turns each one into a signed record with a transcript, then gets it an independent trusted timestamp. You can [verify any record](/compliance-records/verify-a-vcon) yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on QA when you want scores">
    QA is optional and paid. It reviews each call against your scorecard. See [turn on QA](/call-qa/turn-on-qa).
  </Step>
</Steps>
