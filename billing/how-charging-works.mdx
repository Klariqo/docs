---
title: "How charging works"
description: "How Klariqo charges for recorded calls: per call, what counts as a call, what is free, and how long calls are priced. Current prices are on the pricing page."
---

Recorded calls are charged per call, from a prepaid balance. Current prices are on [the pricing page](https://klariqo.com/pricing/). This page explains the rules.

## What you pay for

| Your account | What each call gets | How it's charged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Records** (the default) | A signed, timestamped record with a transcript | Your first 10,000 calls are free, once. After that, the record price per call. |
| **QA** | The record, plus a score against your scorecard, and sentiment | The QA price per scored call, with the record included. The free 10,000 doesn't cover scored calls. A call that couldn't be scored is treated as a record. |

## What counts as a call

- **Only conversations are charged.** A call counts when your dialer's status for it shows a conversation.
- **Legs of one call are one call.** A transfer is grouped with the call it came from, when Klariqo can tell they belong together.
- **Anything unclear isn't charged.** If Klariqo can't tell what happened on a call, the record is still made and the charge is $0.
- **A dial status Klariqo doesn't recognize isn't charged.** See below.

## Short and long calls

| Call length | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Under 10 seconds | Free, and it doesn't use up your free calls |
| 10 to 30 seconds | Half price |
| 31 seconds to 5 minutes | The price per call |
| Over 5 minutes | The price per call, plus a per-minute rate in 30-second blocks |

## On QA, you only pay for a score

A call is charged at the QA price only if it has a real score and arrived after you turned QA on. A call that couldn't be scored, such as a voicemail or a call with no engagement, is treated as a record instead.

## Dial statuses Klariqo doesn't recognize

VICIdial lets you create your own dial statuses. A status is a label your agent chooses, so Klariqo doesn't assume what a new one means. Those calls are recorded and signed, and they aren't charged. They're listed on your **Billing** page so you can see them.

## When charges appear

A call is settled about an hour after it finishes, so all of its legs can arrive first. Your balance changes then, not at the moment of the call.
