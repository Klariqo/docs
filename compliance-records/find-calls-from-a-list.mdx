---
title: "Find calls from a list"
description: "A buyer sent you phone numbers or lead IDs to check. Paste the list into Klariqo, see which calls have records, and hand over the evidence for each one."
---

When a buyer or auditor questions a batch of calls, they usually send a list of phone numbers or lead IDs. You can find those calls yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list search">
    Go to **QA reviews** and choose **Find calls from a list**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your list">
    Choose **Phone numbers** or **Lead IDs**, and paste up to 1,000, one per line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    You get one result for each line, in your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the records">
    Open a call to download its record, or share its verification link. See [verify a record](/compliance-records/verify-a-vcon).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the results mean

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Found | Klariqo has at least one call matching this exactly. |
| Not found | Klariqo has no record for this. It does not mean the call didn't happen. |
| Not searchable | This value can't be searched, such as a number outside North America. |

## What this can't find

- **Calls Klariqo never received,** for example if the recording never reached us.
- **Calls with no phone number or lead ID captured.** The page shows how many of your records have none.
- **Numbers outside North America.** Only North American numbers can be searched today. Those records still exist; find them another way, such as by lead ID.

Matches are exact on purpose, because a loose match could hand over someone else's record. Only your account's owner can search.

<Warning>
  A record shows what was recorded and that it wasn't altered. It does not prove consent or decide a dispute. What it means for your buyer is for you and your counsel.
</Warning>
